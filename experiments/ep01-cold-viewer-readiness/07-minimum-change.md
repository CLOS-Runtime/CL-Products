# Phase VII — MINIMUM-CHANGE PRESCRIPTION
MARKING: EXPERIMENTAL_EVIDENCE_NOT_GOVERNANCE

No rewrite. Only defects the experiment actually produced. Per the frozen rules, an intervention may cite only: consensus attention flags (≥3/7 friction, ≥2/7 exit risk), checkpoint survival losses, recall failures, criterion medians ≤WEAK, or adversary points **corroborated by viewer data**. Replacement prose is proposed only where a defect cannot be expressed as an instruction.

## The interventions

Ranked by evidence weight. "n" = independent evaluators who encountered the problem.

### 1. CUT — the repeated arithmetic in P11
**Remove:** "$2,000 in rent. / $1,300 for the mortgage. / $700 left." Keep "And that's when I did the obvious arithmetic." and "It felt good."
- **Evidence:** consensus FRICTION **7/7** — the only passage in the script every evaluator flagged. Adversary §1 corroborates. V7: "only 'It felt good' is new." V3: "verbatim re-recitation… ten seconds of zero new information at the one-minute mark."
- **n = 7**
- **Benefit:** removes the script's single unanimous friction point, at the ~1:00 mark where a cold viewer is deciding whether the video moves.
- **Lost:** the beat where the narrator performs the naive calculation. Recovered immediately: P15 re-establishes the same figures as the ledger's opening line, which is where they do work.
- **Cost:** 11 words. **Confidence: HIGH.**

### 2. CUT — the mid-episode deferral in P30
**Remove:** "We'll put numbers around that in episode two."
- **Evidence:** consensus FRICTION **7/7** (plus 1 EXIT_RISK vote). V5: "the script builds its most vivid concrete risk and then declines to quantify it." V1: "lands mid-segment rather than as an end-of-video hook." V4: "the first moment I felt the video hold something back, and a second deferral like it would push me out."
- **n = 7**
- **Benefit:** the promise is not lost — **P60 already makes the identical promise at the structurally correct position**, in more compelling form. This is pure de-duplication of a forward reference.
- **Lost:** nothing the episode does not say better 90 seconds later.
- **Cost:** 8 words. **Confidence: HIGH.** This is the highest benefit-to-cost intervention available.

### 3. COMPRESS + CLARIFY — check four's runway (P38–P40)
**Compress** P38–P39 so P40's admission arrives faster and sits adjacent to its payoff at P41–P42. **Clarify** by renaming the check to match the content it actually delivers — its own text at P41–P42 supplies the subject (the *timing* of the money, not its total).
- **Evidence:** **P40 is the script's highest exit risk — 4/7 EXIT_RISK, 6/7 negative overall.** P39 3/7 FRICTION. **5/7 named P40 as their unprompted CP5 attention dip.** Adversary §2 identifies the same location as the largest pacing defect: "The check is named 'Tenant-Ready Costs' and contains no cost."
- **n = 6**
- **Benefit:** addresses the one place where a majority predicted an actual stop.
- **Lost:** nothing, if P40's refusal is preserved — and it must be. V3, the skeptical evaluator, called it "the most credibility-earning sentence in the script," and V6 and V5 both said the honesty raised their trust in every other number. **Do not cut the refusal; cut the runway to it.**
- **Cost:** ~20 words. **Confidence: HIGH on location, MEDIUM on sufficiency.** Honest limit: compression mitigates but does not fully resolve the defect. A check named for a cost that contains no cost can only be fully fixed by supplying a number the household does not have — which is precisely the fabrication the script refuses — or by renaming it. Renaming is the only resolution available that costs the script nothing it possesses.

### 4. COMPRESS — the underwriting list in P35
**Compress:** "It depended on how much projected rent could count. / What documentation we could produce. / And how the timing of the transactions worked."
- **Evidence:** consensus FRICTION **5/7**, plus 1 EXIT_RISK (V6). This is the script's only fully numberless, imageless stretch, and it immediately precedes P37, a consensus EXIT_RISK. V3: "the first passage with no numbers at all… where a viewer's thumb starts hovering." V2: "the least transferable material so far."
- **n = 6**
- **Benefit:** shortens the run-up to the P36 recovery line, which every evaluator marked PULL (7/7), and reduces the exposure at P37.
- **Lost:** granularity about what the lender actually said.
- **Sub-threshold note, reported not prescribed:** V6 alone could not parse "how much projected rent could count" — "count toward *what* is never said." Only 1/7, below the finding threshold, so no intervention is prescribed; recorded because it is the single comprehension failure the experiment produced.
- **Cost:** ~18 words. **Confidence: HIGH.**

### 5. MOVE — the disclaimer in P09
**Relocate** "This was our situation. Our file. Not advice for anyone else." out of the P08→P11 rise.
- **Evidence:** consensus FRICTION **6/7**. V2: "defensive disclaimer inserted at the exact moment the reversal is building; brakes a rising beat." V7: "stops the story one beat after the inciting incident." V5: "pure disclaimer, no informational work." Adversary §6 corroborates: it defends against an accusation not yet available.
- **n = 6**
- **Benefit:** removes friction from the segment where curiosity is climbing fastest.
- **Lost:** if CUT, real trust value — V1 recorded at CP2 that it "increases trust," and V3 called this class of language "anti-sales behavior." **That is why this is a MOVE, not a CUT.**
- **Cost:** 0 net words. **Confidence: HIGH on removing it from that position; MEDIUM on destination** — the evidence establishes where it hurts, not where it belongs. P36 already performs the same scoping function later, which is the natural anchor.

### 6. MOVE — lead the result block with its reframe (P51 ↔ P52)
**Reorder** so "Not a verdict. Conditions." precedes "No green. No red."
- **Evidence:** P51 is a consensus EXIT_RISK (2/7), and both evaluators named the identical mechanism. V2: "announcing 'no green, no red' with 1:40 left tells an outcome-seeking viewer the video has no answer, *before* P52 explains why that is the answer." V3: "P52 rescues it, but only if they are still there five seconds later."
- **n = 2** (meets the frozen exit-risk threshold; mechanism identically named by both)
- **Benefit:** the deflating fact stops arriving before its own explanation, at the last moment a viewer can leave.
- **Lost:** the rhetorical setup of stating the bare result first.
- **Cost:** 0 words. **Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH.**

### 7. COMPRESS — the duplicated statement of the five conditions
The five conditions are stated twice: once per check as "Possible — if X" (P24, P32, P44), and again in full at P53. Compress the per-check clauses; keep P53.
- **Evidence:** **C11 Repetition is WEAK 7/7 — the only unanimous negative criterion in the instrument.** Cadence probe 7/7 detected (5 YES, 2 SOMEWHAT). P53 2/7 FRICTION (sub-threshold on its own). Adversary §5: "P53 is a second reading of the five verdicts already delivered."
- **n = 7** on the underlying repetition finding
- **Benefit:** attacks the one criterion the whole panel rated negatively, and thins the cadence that 7/7 detected — without touching the analysis or the finding.
- **Lost:** V5 noted the per-check conditions "carried the informational load" once the colors stopped discriminating. There is a real trade here.
- **Cost:** ~35 words. **Confidence: MEDIUM.** The evidence establishes the duplication clearly but does not determine which instance to drop; P53 is retained because it is the payoff of "Not a verdict. Conditions."

### 8. MOVE (optional) — P27 after P29
**Relocate** the set-aside/reserve definition so the November/December scenario creates the appetite for the distinction.
- **Evidence:** P27 3/7 FRICTION (threshold met) — but **3/7 also marked it PULL**, so the panel is genuinely split. Adversary §5 corroborates the ordering argument.
- **n = 3**
- **Benefit:** V4 and V7 both said the vivid image at P26 had already made the point and the definition read as re-explanation.
- **Lost:** the logical build from concept to scenario.
- **Cost:** 0 words. **Confidence: LOW-MEDIUM — contested within the panel.** Listed for completeness; not part of the minimum set.

## The minimum set

**Interventions 1–6 are the minimum capable of moving the script to READY.** They address all three consensus exit risks (P40, P37 via P35, P51) and both unanimous frictions (P11, P30). Interventions 7 and 8 are supported but not load-bearing.

Total cost of the minimum set: **≈57 words cut, two relocations, zero new substantive prose** — under 5% of the script. With 7 and 8: ≈92 words, ~7.7%.
