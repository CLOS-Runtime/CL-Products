## Override test A — STRUCTURAL_REVISION_REQUIRED

Triggered if ANY holds:

1. *Survival ≤3/7 at any checkpoint.* — No. 7/7 throughout.
2. *FAIL median on C04, C09, C16 or C17.* — No. STRONG, STRONG, ADEQUATE, ADEQUATE.
3. *≥4/7 locating the same structural cause — wrong reveal placement, wrong section order, or a repetitive architecture that cuts alone cannot fix without rebuilding sections.*

Condition 3 requires care, because the cadence finding is genuinely unanimous (7/7 detected a repetitive cadence; 5 YES, 2 SOMEWHAT). It is nonetheless **not triggered**, on three independent grounds:

- **Not wrong reveal placement.** 7/7 continued after the −$90 reveal, 7/7 judged the main question only PARTLY answered by it, and 0/7 reported POST-ANSWER-APPENDIX at any checkpoint. No evaluator proposed moving the reveal.
- **Not wrong section order.** No evaluator proposed reordering the five checks or removing one. The dissatisfaction is with a *repeated closing token*, not with the sequence or the inventory of sections.
- **Cuts can reach it.** What 7/7 identified is that the verdict output is constant — V5: "a scorecard whose output is constant carries zero information." The five conditions are additionally stated twice (once per check, again in full at P53), which is a duplication removable by compression. The remedies the evaluators themselves proposed are bounded: V1 "vary or drop the verdict colour"; V5 "cutting the second and third restatements." One proposed remedy (V7: "giving one of the five checks a resolved color") *would* require rebuilding the analysis — but it is a single evaluator, below any threshold, and it would change the script's substantive finding, which this experiment has no evidence-based warrant to do.

Counter-consideration recorded: V3, the evaluator most alert to structural manipulation, argued the uniformity is load-bearing — "the uniformity is also what makes 'five yellows' legible as a result, so it costs momentum and buys the ending." This cuts against, not toward, structural revision.

**NOT TRIGGERED.**
