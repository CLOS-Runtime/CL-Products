PREDICTION: A line-item breakdown of true rental economics that shrinks the $700 substantially (possibly to near zero), then a comparison against selling — equity released, maybe the capital-gains exclusion clock — ending either with their decision or, given "We're documenting one real housing decision," a partial answer that sets up future episodes. Risk I'm watching for: staying anecdotal and never generalizing the framework so I can apply it to my own house.
