# Phase IV — RECALL AGGREGATE
MARKING: EXPERIMENTAL_EVIDENCE_NOT_GOVERNANCE

Administered after full exposure, without re-showing the script, before the attention-map round re-displayed the text. Evaluators were told to answer from immediate impression and to mark anything only reconstructable-by-reasoning as PARTIAL/LOST.

**Limitation recorded at freeze time:** evaluator agents retain transcript context; literal forgetting cannot be enforced. These results measure immediate-impression retention plus each evaluator's judgment of what a typical one-view watcher under their condition would still hold. Scored conservatively. Treat as directional, not as a memory experiment on humans.

## The single systematic recall failure: item 3

Every evaluator retained that there were five risks and retained a subset. The consistent pattern is which subset survives and which collapses:

- **Survives across all seven:** the reserve/worst-month risk (carried by the November/December image), the next-mortgage kill condition, and "real costs eat the margin."
- **Collapses in 5/7:** tenant-ready costs and personal capacity blur together into a single vague item. V1: "make-ready and capacity blurring together." V2: "tenant-ready costs and capacity fade." V4: "the last two blur into 'getting it ready and having the time.'" V5: "the make-ready and capacity checks blur together." V7: PARTIAL on tenant-ready specifically, "it arrived without a number and blurs."

The two checks that lost their distinctness in memory are exactly the two checks that contain no arithmetic (checks four and five) — the same two flagged in the attention data. The mechanism the evaluators name is consistent: a check with no number and no scene has nothing to attach to.

## What was uniformly NOT retained (and was not required to be)

All seven independently reported the same losses: the intermediate line items ($350 management, $167 vacancy, $125 maintenance, $150 capex), the $5,000 reserve checkpoint, and the $1,850 rent-sensitivity variant. Per the frozen instrument these are not scored as failures — the underlying mechanism ("a stack of operating costs consumed the margin"; "rent is the softest input") was retained in every case. Three evaluators noted the ~3% mortgage rate survives only as "a rate too cheap to give up."

## Mental-model verdict

The episode produced a **usable mental model rather than mere sentence-by-sentence comprehension**. What survived one exposure in all seven cases is a compressed, correct, and transferable structure: an attractive gross number, a stack of costs that inverts its sign, a set of conditions rather than a verdict, selling as the baseline, and one named open question for next time. The one structural weakness is that the five-item risk list does not survive as five distinct items in five of seven cases, and it degrades along a predictable axis — the unquantified checks are the ones that fade.
